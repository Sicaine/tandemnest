# How TandemNest makes money

This page lists every monetised relationship on the site, whether it is currently active
or not. It is deliberately more detailed than a disclosure needs to be, because the
comparison pages here are only worth reading if you can see exactly where the incentive
sits.

## Why the payout kind is listed

Referral programmes pay in very different currencies, and the difference matters to a
reader trying to judge bias:

- **Account credit** can only be spent with the provider that issued it. It creates an incentive to keep sending traffic to that same provider.
- **Cash commission** is ordinary revenue.
- **Recurring commission** pays a share of the referred customer's spend over time, which creates an incentive for the recommendation to actually work out for you, not merely to get you to sign up.

Where a provider offers both a credit-paying referral tier and a cash-paying affiliate
tier, the table says which one is in use.

## The rules applied to monetised pages

1. **The recommendation follows the data, not the payout.** Where the cheapest or best option has no referral programme, it is still named first. Several of the providers recommended on this site pay nothing at all.
2. **Referral links are labelled inline**, next to the link, not in a footer. They carry `rel="sponsored nofollow"`.
3. **No provider gets pre-publication review.** None has been contacted for approval, sent a draft, or given editorial input.
4. **No paid placement.** Position in any table is determined by the data in that table, usually by price.
5. **Removal test.** Before a page is published, it is checked against a simple question: with every referral link stripped out, is this page still worth the reader's time? If not, the page does not go up.
6. **No fabricated evidence.** No invented benchmarks, no invented reviews, no star ratings, no "editor's choice" badges, and no review markup in the structured data.

## What is deliberately not done here

- No display advertising.
- No email list, newsletter capture, or exit popups.
- No analytics scripts, tracking pixels, fingerprinting, or cookies. The site sets none.
- No sponsored posts presented as independent research.
- No link cloaking or redirect chains. Every outbound link shows its real destination.
- No cryptocurrency mining, in the browser or otherwise, and no wallet interaction of any kind.
